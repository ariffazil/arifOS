*outcome* (PARTIAL falls between HOLD and SEAL) but wrong in *shape*
(PARTIAL is its own state, not a flag on SEAL).

  exactly what `llms.txt` and `arif_judge` emit.

  SABAR > PARTIAL > SEAL`) and treat all other shapes as deprecated
