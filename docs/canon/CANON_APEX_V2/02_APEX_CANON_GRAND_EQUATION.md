### Why E is one letter, not two

Entropy and energy are not two constructs forced into a marriage — they are a
thermodynamic conjugate pair. The Landauer bound:

```
ΔE ≥ kT · ln2 · ΔS
```

The cost of changing information IS energy times entropy. One physical dial,
two faces. This is stronger than the 6-dial split, not a compromise.

### Why P carries Authority

State-truth and legitimacy travel together in every governed decision:
"what is the world?" and "who may change it?" are answered at the same gate.
F13 SOVEREIGN lives here and only here.

```
G = geomean(A, P, E, E, X)  =  (A · P · E² · X)^(1/5)
```

Properties:

1. **Scale-true.** Every dial ∈ [0,1] ⇒ G ∈ [0,1]. Thresholds interpret on

4. **Mnemonic-true.** The letters spell APEX: AKAL · PRESENT · ENTROPY×ENERGY ·
   EXPLORATION×AMANAH.

**A: AKAL — Lawful Reasoning**
- Confidence ≠ evidence. Coherence ≠ truth.
- Every claim carries OBS/DER/INT/SPEC label.
- Confidence cap = 1 − Ω₀ ∈ [0.95, 0.97].

**P: PRESENT — Attested Live State**
- Every fact has source: LIVE / CACHED / INFERRED.
- INFERRED cannot authorize IRREVERSIBLE.
- Sense before act. Probe before claim.

**H: AUTHORITY — Legitimacy** *(scored inside P)*
- Every actor is a registered principal.
- Every action requires role permission.
- F13 sovereign veto absolute — machine cannot override.

**S: ENTROPY — Uncertainty Integrity** *(scored inside E)*
- Unknowns must be declared with Ω₀ band.

**E: ENERGY — Thermodynamic Adequacy** *(scored inside E)*
- Compute cost must match action reversibility.
- Wasteful computation → E dial collapse (E² penalty in G).
- Landauer bound respected per bit transition.
