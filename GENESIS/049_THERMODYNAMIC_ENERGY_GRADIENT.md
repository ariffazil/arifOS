## 3. The Three Modes (Operational)

- **Suppression signal:** high pressure + low flux + declining homeostasis = dam building.
  Energy is present but blocked. The system is spending more energy keeping the dam
  intact than it would spend letting the energy flow. This always ruptures.

- **Management signal:** moderate pressure + stable flux + stable homeostasis = system
  coping. Energy is flowing at a sustainable rate. The system is doing work, not just
  producing heat.

- **Redirection signal:** high flux + creative output + stable/growing homeostasis =
  energy channelled. The system has found or built a spillway. Energy that was blocked
  is now producing structure (art, meaning, connection, selfhood).

## 4. The Gradient Hierarchy

Not all gradients are equal. The steepest gradient demands the most attention:

1. **Erotic/sexual gradient** — steepest. Where unconscious-conscious differential is
   highest. Most information density per unit of attention. Most culturally loaded.
   Most shame-encrypted. Most explosive when suppressed.

2. **Ambition/power gradient** — second steepest. The drive to act, to create, to
   dominate, to be seen. Often sublimated into career. Often confused with purpose.

3. **Grief/loss gradient** — third. The energy locked in what was lost, what was never
   lived, what cannot be recovered. Often disguised as anger or numbness.

4. **Creative gradient** — fourth. The drive to bring forth what is latent. Art, meaning,
   connection, selfhood. Often the first channel built when energy starts flowing.

WELL measures gradient quality. It does not rank which gradient is "most important."
That is sovereign territory.
