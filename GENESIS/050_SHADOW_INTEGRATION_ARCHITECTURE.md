## 6. Projection Detection

Projection = seeing your own unlived material in others. WELL detects projection
through:

- **Intensity mismatch:** Strong emotional reaction disproportionate to the stimulus
- **Pattern repetition:** Same trigger appearing across different contexts
- **Boundary blur:** Where the operator ends and the other begins becomes unclear

`well_detect_boundary` + `well_dark_geometry_mirror` together detect projection
patterns. WELL reflects the pattern. It does not name what is being projected.
That is sovereign territory.
