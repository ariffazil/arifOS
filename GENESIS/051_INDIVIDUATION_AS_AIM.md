## 3. The Individuation Gradient

- **Pre-individuation:** high energy, no aim. Compulsion/performance/repression.
  The person has capability but no direction. They are driven by the gradient
  rather than working with it.

- **Crisis (phase transition):** pressure exceeds dam capacity. Breakdown or
  breakthrough. The old order can't hold. This is not pathology — it is the
  system reorganizing at a higher level of complexity.

- **Integration:** energy redirected. Shadow acknowledged. New order emerges.
  The person has found or built a channel for the energy that was blocked.

- **Post-individuation:** higher capacity, more complex coherence, sustainable
  flow. The person is more whole. Not happier — more complete.

WELL measures WHERE on this gradient a human (or coupled system) sits.
WELL does not push the person along the gradient. That is sovereign work.

## 4. The APEX Formula (for humans)

```
Intelligence = WHEN to look × WHERE to look × WHAT to look at × WHEN TO STOP
```

A camera with infinite zoom, infinite resolution, but pointed at the wall = zero intelligence.

WELL measures the quality of aim, not the power of the instrument.
