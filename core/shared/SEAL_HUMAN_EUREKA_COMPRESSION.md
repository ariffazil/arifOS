- **Wrong:** Human = personality.

- **Trait** is merely the current policy snapshot.

### 2. Personality is visible architecture.
```
Scar → Tension → Regulation Strategy → Architecture → Behavior
```

### 3. Character = recurring governance policy.
- Not what they think. Not what they say.
- **What they do repeatedly when tension appears.**

### 4. Shadow is not evil. Shadow is expensive.
- Shadow = **high-cost territory** (expensive self), not forbidden self.

- Order ↔ Freedom, Truth ↔ Comfort, Control ↔ Surrender, Power ↔ Love, Autonomy ↔ Connection.

### 7. Trauma does not determine outcome.
- Same scar → Builder, Mapper, Protector, Container, Teacher, Caretaker.
- **Scar ≠ destiny.** Architecture built after scar matters.

### 8. Map through tensions, not labels.
- Don't ask: "Who is he?"
- Ask: **"What tension is he regulating?"**
- Reduces projection. Increases accuracy.

### 9. Attraction occurs at architecture level.
- Not hobbies. Not beliefs. Not personality.
- **Mutual recognition of invisible burdens.**
- "I see your scar shape. It matches mine."

### 10. Intelligence = Selector function.
- Not answers. **Position selection.**
- Choosing Truth or Comfort, Order or Freedom, Control or Surrender based on **reality semasa**.

### 11. Wisdom is not staying on one side.
- Not always Truth. Not always Love.
- **Right position. Right context. Right time.**
- **Governance.**

### 12. THE FINAL SEAL
People are not what they say.
People are not what they feel.
People are not what they want.

**People are recurring solutions to recurring contradictions.**
