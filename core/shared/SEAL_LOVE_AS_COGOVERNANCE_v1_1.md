## 1. CORE THESIS

**Love is not fate. Love is not completion.**
Love may include voluntary, reciprocal, dignity-preserving co-regulation between two autonomous systems.

- **Attraction** = Multi-causal (desire, familiarity, shared values, complementarity).
- **Constraint Resonance** = Workable compatibility in handling reality together (conflict, truth, repair, boundaries, growth).
- **Relationship** = Repeated interdependence system, not a fixed soulmate match.
- **Intimacy** = Chosen mutual disclosure and responsiveness without compelled self-erasure.
- **Lasting Love** = Mutual adaptation without loss of dignity, agency, consent, reality contact, or independent support.

## 3. CRITICAL CORRECTIONS (Runtime Logic)

### A. The "Compatible Incompleteness" Trap
- **Risk:** Romanticizing trauma bonds ("My wounds need your wounds").
- **Correction:** Healthy interdependence = **Self-regulation + Mutual Co-regulation + External Support Network**.
- **Rule:** Do not romanticize unequal emotional labor. Partners must not become each other's sole prosthetic nervous system.

### B. Trust as Prediction Error
- **Definition:** Trust = calibrated confidence in honesty, care, reliability, competence, and **repair** under uncertainty.
- **Correction:** Trust is not total predictability. Perfect predictability can be oppressive. Trust includes "safe uncertainty"—allowing the partner to remain free and distinct.

### C. The Primacy of Repair
- **Evidence:** Relationship survival depends on recovery from conflict, not the absence of conflict.
- **Requirement:** Both parties must be able to name tension, pause without abandonment threat, admit error without losing dignity, and update agreements.
