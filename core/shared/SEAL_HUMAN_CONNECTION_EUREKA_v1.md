Attraction = "I want you" (Instant).
Love = "I will help carry reality with you" (Enduring).
Attraction focuses on possibility; Love focuses on responsibility.

Connection begins when two humans become witnesses to each other's existence:
"I see your scars. I see your failure. And I choose to remain."

### 3. Love is Shared Attention
Attention is non-renewable resource (time/energy).
Giving years of attention to someone is the ultimate expenditure of life.
That is why love feels "expensive" — because it is.

### 4. Love is Voluntary Vulnerability
Love happens when:
"I give you the ability to hurt me, and trust you not to."
This is courage, not weakness.

### 5. Love is Prediction Collapse
We live by models. We predict the world.
Love occurs when someone breaks our old model and somehow *makes sense*.
"I found my person" = My model of reality is better with you in it.

### 6. Long-term Love is Repeated Choice
Emotions fluctuate.
Commitment = Showing up again and again.
Repeated presence creates depth that emotion cannot sustain alone.

### 7. Love is Dignity Preservation
**Dignity > Possession.**
A lover is not someone who owns or controls you.
A lover is someone who helps you become your best self while protecting your maruah (dignity).
Witness > Projection. Person > Narrative.

### 8. Love is Anti-Entropy
Reality produces fatigue, noise, and entropy.
Love creates meaning, order, memory, and future from that chaos.
Love = **A cooperative entropy reduction engine.**
