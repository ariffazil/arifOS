## Stance on retained terms (acknowledged but unchanged)

**Entropy (Shannon).** When the underlying distribution is defined and the log base is chosen, Shannon entropy is a legitimate measure. We retain `well_measure_gradient` and `well_compute_metabolic_flux` because they explicitly ship with their distributions. We do not retain "governance entropy" because no distribution is defined.

**Metabolism.** When the underlying substrate is biological, the term is precise. WELL `well_assess_metabolism` is biologically grounded. We do not retain "machine metabolism" — that's `well_assess_reliability`.

**Homeostasis.** Same as metabolism: biological foundation, keep.

**Field, gradient.** In a defined mathematical space (state space, risk field), these are precise. Keep.

**Civilisation.** Civilisational-scale work needs a word. Civilisation is fine. "Civilisation Intelligence = APEX in disguise" is not.

**Substrate.** Federated substrates (biological, machine, institutional, coupled). Keep. Substrate classification is the G-WELL immune system.
