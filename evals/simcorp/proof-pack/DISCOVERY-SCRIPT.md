## Minggu 4 — hantar cadangan berbayar (blok dari audit)

```
Scope:      Govern one AI-driven action family.
Controls:   Identity verification, action schema validation, policy
            thresholds, scope-limited approval, denial/block rules,
            action receipts.
Duration:   Four weeks.
Success:    Defined unsafe actions do not execute; routine eligible
            actions remain autonomous; all decisions traceable;
            customer can verify enforcement.
Exclusions: No replacement of IAM/SIEM/ERP/GRC. No compliance
            certification claim. No unmanaged direct access path
            outside the governed connector.
Price:      RM20k–50k (bayaran bukti pemilikan bajet — bukan percuma)
```
