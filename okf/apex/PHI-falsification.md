# Φ — Falsification Rate

**Type bridge:** EXPLORATION × AKAL × ENERGY_ENTROPY

GEOX owns this dial. Every geological interpretation is a falsifiable hypothesis. Φ measures how many have been tested and eliminated.

- EXPLORATION: generate hypotheses
- AKAL: reason about what would falsify them
- ENERGY_ENTROPY: false models dissipate — they cost real resources

A high Φ means the system has eliminated bad models. A low Φ means speculation is piling up untested.
